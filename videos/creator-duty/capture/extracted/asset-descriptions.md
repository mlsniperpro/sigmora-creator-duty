# Captured product surfaces

The live page is the visual source of truth for this show-it-as-is demo. The
capture produced no standalone image or logo assets; the usable visual assets
are the page screenshots below.

- `capture/screenshots/full-page.png` — full-page Creator Duty judging panel;
  dark Sigmora surface with the hero promise, workflow stages, campaign plan,
  immutable vertical promo, release policy, receipts, trace, variants, and
  recap.
- `capture/screenshots/scroll-000.png` — top viewport; product name, hero
  message, event controls, system pills, metrics, and workflow strip.
- `capture/screenshots/scroll-037.png` — upper-middle viewport; event, Gemini
  plan, vertical promo, policy checks, and destination receipts.
- `capture/screenshots/scroll-074.png` — lower-middle viewport; policy detail,
  correlated agent activity, and channel variants.
- `capture/screenshots/scroll-100.png` — closing viewport; agent activity,
  channel variants, post-live recap, question clusters, and disclosure footer.
- `capture/assets/` — reserved for any staged source media; no separate assets
  were discovered by the capture.

The screenshots are captured from the deployed Cloud Run judging panel. The
panel itself labels synthetic events, deterministic media, and sandbox
destinations; the video must preserve those labels.
